Who I Am

I'm a detail-oriented Associate Software Engineer at The Boeing Company, based in Bengaluru, India. I build and deploy mission-critical backend services for some of the world's most advanced aerospace systems.

My work spans the full software lifecycle — from architecting Java Spring Boot microservices containerized with Docker for onboard aircraft deployment, to leading QA automation frameworks for the 737 MAX and 777-8F programs that directly improve aviation safety.

I graduated with a B.Tech in Information Science from M S Ramaiah Institute of Technology (2022) and immediately joined Boeing where I've been recognized for delivering projects ahead of schedule and significantly boosting testing efficiency.

Beyond work, I'm passionate about cloud-native technologies, having earned 6 Google Cloud certifications, and I love building personal full-stack projects that solve real problems.
